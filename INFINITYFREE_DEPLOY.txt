MEDCARE - HUONG DAN UPLOAD LEN INFINITYFREE

1. Giai nen ZIP.
2. Upload toan bo noi dung trong thu muc htdocs/ len thu muc htdocs cua InfinityFree.
   Khong upload thu muc laravel/ vao ben trong htdocs/ rieng le.
   ZIP da tach Laravel vao laravel/ de public/index.php co the truy cap dung autoload.
3. Chon PHP sang phien ban tuong thuc voi Laravel (php artisan/phien ban hop le).
   Composer cua du an yeu cau PHP 8.5; InfinityFree phai cung cap PHP 8.5 hoac phien ban
   tuong thich voi dependencies duoc dong goi trong thu muc laravel/vendor.
4. Tao database MySQL trong InfinityFree va tao tai khoan database.
5. Doi ten laravel/.env.example thanh laravel/.env.
6. Cap nhat laravel/.env:
   APP_NAME="MedCare"
   APP_ENV=production
   APP_DEBUG=false
   APP_URL=https://ten-mien-cua-ban
   DB_CONNECTION=mysql
   DB_HOST=ten-host-mysql
   DB_PORT=3306
   DB_DATABASE=ten-database
   DB_USERNAME=ten-user
   DB_PASSWORD=mat-khau
   DB_PREFIX=medcare_
   SESSION_DRIVER=file
   FILESYSTEM_DISK=local
   QUEUE_CONNECTION=sync
   CACHE_STORE=file
7. Tao APP_KEY bang terminal neu host ho tro Artisan. Neu khong co terminal, chay
   local: php artisan key:generate --show, roi dien gia tri vao APP_KEY.
8. Chay migration bang terminal neu host ho tro. Neu khong ho tro terminal, can nạp
   SQL migration bang phpMyAdmin hoac tim host Laravel ho tro SSH/Composer.

Luu y: file public/ va laravel/storage duoc bao gom trong ZIP.
Khong upload .git, node_modules hoac thong tin bi mat.
